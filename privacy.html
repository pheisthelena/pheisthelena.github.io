Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

What data the app accesses

deadline-bot uses Google sign-in to get read-only access to Google Classroom data of the account that signs in: the list of courses, and the titles, links and due dates of assignments in these courses. Only the developer of the bot signs in, with the developer's own account.

How the data is used

The data is used for one purpose: to build a list of upcoming homework deadlines and show it in the Telegram chats chosen by the developer (the developer's private chat and the study group's chat).

Storage

The bot stores an access token for the Google account on its server so that it can read the deadlines automatically. It does not store Classroom content permanently: the list is rebuilt from Google Classroom on each check. The bot does not read, change or delete any other Google data.

Sharing

The data is not sold and is not shared with third parties. Assignment titles, links and due dates are shown only in the Telegram chats mentioned above.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that the bot can no longer read any Google Classroom data.

deadline-bot

deadline-bot is a small personal Telegram bot for a university study group. It collects upcoming homework deadlines in one list and posts them to the group's Telegram chat.

What it does

  • Reads the titles and due dates of assignments from the developer's own Google Classroom courses (read-only).
  • Combines them with deadlines from other course pages and deadlines added by hand.
  • Shows the list in Telegram on request and announces new deadlines.

Who can use it

The Google sign-in is used only by the developer of the bot, with the developer's own Google account. The bot is a non-commercial student project.
